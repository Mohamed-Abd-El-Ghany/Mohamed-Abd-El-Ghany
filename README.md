## 👋 About me

I'm a Flutter developer based in **Cairo, Egypt**. I build complete iOS and Android apps, from the first screen to the store listing, and I've shipped **25+ apps** that are live on the App Store and Google Play today.

- 📱 Currently a **Flutter Developer at ATHR**, where I built **Ofuq**, a kids learning app, from scratch
- 🧱 I work with **Clean Architecture, BLoC and SOLID**, so apps stay easy to grow and maintain
- 🤖 I add **AI features** with Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini: chatbots, assistants and planners
- ⚙️ I also build the **backend and admin dashboard** with Firebase or Supabase
- 🚀 I handle **store publishing** end to end, on both stores
- 🎓 Mansoura University graduate
- 🌍 I work in Arabic and English
